**The Meridian** — one page summing up Wednesday 25 November 2026, drawn by `main.py` with PIL into a 1024×1024 front page.

Lead story: China opens a corruption probe into Shanghai party chief Chen Jining, the fourth Politburo member investigated since 2025. Also inside: the WHO evacuation from an Ebola hospital in Congo, the UN's femicide figures, the Huawei verdict, Kyiv's Baltic ship strike, the Houthi strike on Riyadh's PIF Tower, Giuliani's Medal of Freedom, and Shein's $100m FTC settlement. Every story was verified against at least two news sources reporting on 25 November 2026.

Run it with `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py` — it writes `entry.png`, deterministically, twice the same bytes. A built-in validation step checks the 1024×1024 size, the 1 MiB cap, headline fit, and lead-headline legibility at the judges' 300px test size.
